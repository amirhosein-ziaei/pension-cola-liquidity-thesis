# G-P1 research question (as proposed, not redesigned)

## Exact question

Do cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) formulas established before the 2021–23 inflation surge increase pension benefit cash outflows and induce sales of liquid assets, especially among pension systems with high pre-existing illiquid asset exposure?

Source: `Final thesis adjudication October 2026.md`, candidate table (G-P1 row) and section "G P1 Pension liabilities as a conditional reserve".

## Proposed mechanism

1. A plan's COLA rule (CPI share, cap, floor, compounding, waiting period, benefit base) is fixed before 2021.
2. The 2021–23 inflation surge maps that rule into a larger nominal benefit increase for plans whose rules pass more inflation through (high or no cap, CPI-linked) than for plans with fixed-rate or low-cap COLAs.
3. Larger benefit payments widen negative net operating cash flow (benefits − contributions).
4. The investment pool must raise cash. Under the hypothesis, systems with larger pre-surge illiquid (alternatives) shares sell more liquid assets (public equity, fixed income) per dollar of outflow.

## Claimed contribution

Use predetermined benefit-indexation rules as a source of **liability-side** cash pressure, instead of statistical cash-flow residuals, to estimate liquid-asset disposals per dollar of liability-driven outflow, by pre-surge illiquidity.

## Estimand (from the adjudication report)

Liquid net disposals per dollar of COLA-induced benefit cash flow, at system-year frequency, with prespecified heterogeneity by pre-surge illiquid share. A benefit first stage (rule-implied versus actual benefit growth) must be shown before any sales result is interpreted.

## What this Phase 0 audit does not do

It does not redesign the question, build a dataset or estimate anything. The only quantitative work is a documentation-level profile of the Public Plans Data (PPD) COLA rule table and income-statement coverage (`outputs/data_feasibility_precheck.md`), plus a back-of-envelope power scaling from the competitor's published table (`outputs/identification_risks.md`).
